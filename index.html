Welcome, and thanks for stopping by. I am a postdoctoral researcher at the IITB–Citadel Securities Quantitative Research Lab at IIT Bombay, a position I began in August 2026. I completed my Ph.D. in Mathematics at IIT Guwahati in June 2026.

My research develops learning methods for noisy, time-dependent data and decision-making. I work on randomized neural networks, deep ensembles, time-series forecasting, and uncertainty-aware models, with applications in quantitative finance, energy, and risk analysis.

My current work brings together statistical learning and financial market modelling, including volatility and risk forecasting, algorithmic trading, portfolio decisions, and survival analysis for decentralized finance.

Explore my research areas, browse my publications, or get in touch.

Thesis title: Learning Frameworks for Time Series Analysis and Financial Decision-Making using Backpropagation and Randomized Neural Networks.

Supervisors: Prof. Selvaraju Natarajan (Department of Mathematics, IIT Guwahati) and Prof. Ponnuthurai Nagaratnam Suganthan (Department of Computer Science and Engineering, Qatar University).

Methods & toolkit

01

Build expressive learners

Combine randomized RVFL and deep RVFL networks, with fitted output layers, and backpropagation-trained deep models to learn nonlinear representations efficiently.

02

Model temporal structure

Use LSTM/GRU recurrent networks, transformer-inspired and state-space architectures, and multi-output learning for sequential and long-horizon data. Apply systematic factor mining to discover and evaluate candidate financial signals.

03

Quantify risk & develop decisions

Apply heteroscedastic models and deep ensembles to changing variance and forecast uncertainty; use deep reinforcement learning (DRL) for trading and portfolio decisions, and explore large language models (LLMs) and agentic systems for research and evaluation workflows.

Implementation toolkit: Python, R, C, MATLAB, LaTeX, TensorFlow, PyTorch, Hugging Face Transformers, Joblib, Dask, high-performance computing, and parallel computing.
