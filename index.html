Welcome, and thanks for stopping by. I am a PhD candidate in mathematics at the Indian Institute of Technology Guwahati. My research focuses on deep learning based modelling for noisy time-dependent data and decision making.

I work on randomized neural architectures, ensemble methods, time series forecasting, and uncertainty-aware predictive modelling. My interests lie at the intersection of statistical learning, applied mathematics, machine learning, and quantitative finance.

I am also interested in developing reliable models that remain useful under noise, uncertainty, and distribution shift. You can learn more about my research, publications, or academic background.

Research

My current work explores deep learning methods for complex time-dependent systems, with particular emphasis on:

  • Randomized neural networks and ensemble learning
  • Time series analysis and forecasting
  • Uncertainty-aware predictive modelling
  • Quantitative finance and statistical learning
